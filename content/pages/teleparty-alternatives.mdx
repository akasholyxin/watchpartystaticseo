## The two kinds of watch party tools

Before comparing names, it helps to know there are two different approaches:

1. **Sync tools** (Teleparty, Watch Party, Scener): everyone opens the video on their own account, and the tool keeps play, pause and seek in step. The picture is full quality for everyone, but everyone needs access to what you're watching.
2. **Shared-room tools** (Hyperbeam, WatchParty.me's virtual browser, Kosmi's shared browsing, Watch2Gether's room player): the room plays one browser or player that everyone watches. Easy for links and YouTube, and some work on phones, but you're watching the room's stream rather than your own.

## 1. Watch Party: free calls on any site

A Chrome extension that keeps playback in sync on any website with a standard video player, with **group video and voice calls, chat and reactions** in a side panel, all free.

- **Best for:** seeing each other while you watch, on Netflix, YouTube or any other site
- **Not for:** phones, TVs, Safari or Firefox (desktop Chrome only)

## 2. Watch2Gether: quick rooms for YouTube links

Create a room, paste a link, and everyone watches in a shared, synchronized player. Strongest with YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion and SoundCloud. No account needed, and it works in mobile browsers.

- **Best for:** YouTube nights and groups on phones
- **Less ideal for:** watching your own streaming accounts at full quality

## 3. Kosmi: hangouts with integrations

A free room for movie and game nights with voice, video and chat built in. It has integrations for services like Netflix and YouTube, plus shared browsing and screen sharing.

- **Best for:** mixed hangouts (games, videos, chat) and mobile users

## 4. Hyperbeam: a shared browser on their servers

Everyone in the room uses one "multiplayer" browser that runs in the cloud, with chat and calls.

- **Best for:** browsing sites together, or when not everyone has the same account

## 5. WatchParty.me: virtual browser, files and URLs

Rooms with a cloud browser, YouTube, screen sharing, file upload or a direct video URL, plus video chat. No registration, and there's a Discord bot to create rooms.

- **Best for:** Discord groups and playing your own files

## 6. Scener: watch major streaming platforms in sync

Scener describes itself as a free way to watch shows and movies socially from major streaming platforms in sync.
