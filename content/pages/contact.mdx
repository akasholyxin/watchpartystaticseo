## Email

[watchpartyapplive@gmail.com](mailto:watchpartyapplive@gmail.com)

We read every message. A two-line email about what went wrong genuinely helps us decide what to fix next.

## Inside the extension

Open the extension's settings and choose **Help & Support** to send us a message without leaving Chrome.

## Before you write

- **Sync problem?** Try the [troubleshooting guide](/blog/watch-party-not-syncing/) first. Most issues are fixed in a minute.
- **Question about features?** The [FAQ](/faq/) covers the common ones.

## Reporting a bug

Tell us:

1. What you were watching (the site, not the title)
2. What you expected and what happened
3. Whether you were the host or a guest

Please don't send passwords or account details.
