## The online setup, in short

Everyone installs {{brand}} in desktop Chrome, the host opens the video and presses **Create Watch Party**, friends join with the room code, and playback stays in sync for everyone. Every click is in [how it works](/how-it-works/).

<Install position="mid" />

## Hosting in one room instead?

For a match or a season finale at home: test the stream and sound an hour before, put the screen where everyone can see it, plan food you can eat without looking down, and agree on the "no spoilers on phones" rule. If some friends can't come, run an online room at the same time so they can join from home.
