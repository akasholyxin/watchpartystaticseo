## Episode nights, done right

Agree on a series, set a weekly slot, and let the host drive: skip the opening, pause for theories, and run straight into the next episode together. Keep chat for running commentary and save the call for the cliffhanger.

Use the anime service you already have. {{brand}} doesn't provide or stream any video; it keeps your own playback in step with your friends'.

## How to run a weekly anime night

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| Pick the show | Choose something everyone can stream on their own account and in their country |
| Set the pace | Two to four episodes a night keeps it fun without burning out |
| Agree on subs or dub | Everyone sets it on their own player before the host presses play |
| Host drives | The host skips openings and recaps; everyone else follows automatically |
| Theory break | Pause between episodes for five minutes of predictions on the call |
| Next week | End on the cliffhanger so people come back |

## What works well with a group

- **Short episodic comedies:** easy to drop into, lots to react to
- **Mysteries:** pause for theories, then see who was right
- **Big action arcs and finales:** best with the video call on so you see the reactions
- **Rewatches of a favorite series:** let the person who loves it host
