## Why we made it

Watching something with friends who live far away usually meant counting "3… 2… 1… play" on a call, or squinting at a blurry screen share. We wanted movie night to feel like being on the same couch again: press play once, everyone watches together, and you can see and hear each other while you do.

## What it does

- **Synced playback** on any site with a standard video player
- **Group video and voice calls, chat and reactions** in a side panel
- **Public and private rooms**, friends and direct messages
- **Free**, and guests don't need an account

See [how it works](/how-it-works/) for every step, or [download it for Chrome](/download/).
