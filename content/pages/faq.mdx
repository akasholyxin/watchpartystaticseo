faq:
  - q: "Is it free?"
    a: "Yes. Creating rooms, syncing video, chat and video calls are all free."
  - q: "Do I need an account?"
    a: "No. You can start or join a watch party as a guest. Signing in with Google only adds friends, direct messages and settings that follow you across devices."
  - q: "Which browsers does it work in?"
    a: "Desktop Chrome."
  - q: "Which sites does it work on?"
    a: "Websites with a standard HTML5 video player, including the major streaming services, YouTube, Twitch, anime sites and more."
  - q: "Do we need the same subscription?"
    a: "Yes. Everyone plays the video from their own account, so each person needs access to what you're watching. That's also why the quality stays perfect for everyone."
  - q: "How many people can watch together?"
    a: "The host sets the room capacity for each room, up to 500 people."
  - q: "Can we video call while we watch?"
    a: "Yes. Group video and voice calls are built into the side panel, along with chat and reactions."
  - q: "Can I watch with friends in another country?"
    a: "Yes. Everyone streams on their own connection, so distance doesn't affect quality. The title needs to be available in each person's region."
  - q: "Can strangers join my room?"
    a: "Only if you make it public. Private rooms are invite-only."
  - q: "Do you see or record what I watch?"
    a: "No. Only a timestamp and play or pause state are synced. The video is never captured or re-streamed. The address of the page your room is watching is shared with people in that room."
  - q: "Is a watch party like this allowed?"
    a: "Each person watches on their own account, and nobody's video is copied, recorded or re-streamed. {{brand}} only keeps play, pause and the timestamp in sync. Each service's own terms still apply to your account."
  - q: "Why do friends see a black screen when I share Netflix?"
    a: "Netflix and most streaming services protect their video, so screen shares show black. {{brand}} doesn't share your screen: everyone streams the video themselves and only the playback is synced."
  - q: "Does it work on phones, TVs, Safari or Firefox?"
    a: "No. {{brand}} is a Chrome extension for desktop Chrome."
  - q: "Are calls recorded?"
    a: "No. Calls are peer-to-peer between participants and are never recorded or stored on our servers."
  - q: "The video isn't in sync. What do I do?"
    a: "Refresh the page, check everyone has the same video open and is signed in to the service, then rejoin the room. More fixes in our troubleshooting guide."
