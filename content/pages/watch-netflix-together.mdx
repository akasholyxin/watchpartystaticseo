## What you need

- **Desktop Chrome** for each person
- **Your own Netflix account** each. Nobody's account is shared and the video is never re-streamed.
- **The same title in each region** if you're in different countries

## Why screen sharing Netflix shows a black screen

Netflix protects its video, so when you share your screen in Discord, Zoom or Meet, your friends usually see a black box with sound. Even when it does show, a screen share is compressed and choppy. A watch party works differently: everyone streams Netflix themselves, at the quality their plan allows, and only the play, pause and timestamp are kept in step. More in [watch party vs screen sharing](/blog/watch-party-vs-screen-sharing/).

## Using Teleparty?

Teleparty is the best-known Netflix watch party extension. Its free plan includes sync and text chat; voice and video chat are part of its Premium plan.
