## For the host

1. **Open the video** you want to watch.
2. **Click the {{brand}} icon** in your Chrome toolbar. The side panel opens on the right, with five tabs: Room, Explore, Friends, Messages and Profile.
3. On the **Room** tab, press **Create Watch Party**.
4. **Share the room code** with your friends: paste it in your group chat or message.
5. When everyone's in, **press play**. Pausing, skipping and rewinding happen for the whole room.

## For guests

1. **[Add {{brand}} to Chrome](/download/)** (free, no account needed).
2. **Click the icon** to open the side panel.
3. **Paste the room code** into **Paste room ID** and join. You'll land on the same video, in sync with the host.

Make sure you can play the video yourself first: if it's on a subscription service, you need your own account for it.

## While you watch

- **Video and voice calls:** start a call from the panel to see and hear each other.
- **Chat and reactions:** comment without talking over the film, react to the big moments.
- **Everyone stays in step:** joining late lands you on the same video and time.

## No group tonight?

Open the **Explore** tab or press **Browse public rooms** to find a public watch party and join it. You can also make your own room public so others can find it, or keep it invite-only.
