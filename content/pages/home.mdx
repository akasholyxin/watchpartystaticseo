## Why not just share your screen?

Screen sharing sends a compressed copy of one person's screen, so the movie looks blurry, stutters and eats the host's upload. Many streaming services also show a black screen when you try. With {{brand}}, everyone plays the original video on their own connection and only the play, pause and timestamp are kept in step.

## Your video stays yours

New to the idea? Start with [what a watch party is](/blog/what-is-a-watch-party/), or jump to [how to watch Netflix with friends](/blog/how-to-watch-netflix-with-friends/) and [YouTube with friends](/blog/how-to-watch-youtube-with-friends/).

{{brand}} syncs a timestamp and a play or pause state, never your video. Nothing you watch is captured, recorded or re-streamed, calls go directly between participants, and the extension doesn't read your browsing history. The details are in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
