## Two ways to watch online, and why sync wins

Most tools work one of two ways. **Shared-room tools** play one browser or player and stream it to everyone. **Sync tools** like {{brand}} let everyone play the video on their own account, and keep play, pause and seek in step. Sync means full quality for every person and no black screen on protected services. The trade-off: everyone needs access to what you're watching.
