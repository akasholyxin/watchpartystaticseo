There are four ways to watch YouTube with friends at the same time. Which one is best depends on whether you want to stay on YouTube itself, whether anyone's on a phone, and whether your group already lives on Discord.

## Which way should you use?

| Method | Stays on youtube.com | Phones | Call built in | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sync extension | Yes | No | Yes ({{brand}}) | Desktop groups who want to see each other |
| Room sites (paste a link) | No, shared player | Yes | Some | Quick sessions, mixed devices |
| Discord activities | No, inside Discord | Yes | Discord voice/video | Groups already in a voice channel |
| Screen share | No | Yes | Your call app | A single short clip |

## 1. A sync extension (stay on YouTube)

Everyone watches on youtube.com with their own account, and the extension keeps play, pause and timestamps in step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install">Everyone adds {{brand}} to desktop Chrome (free).</Step>
  <Step title="Open the video">The host opens any YouTube video.</Step>
  <Step title="Create a party">Click the icon, press **Create Watch Party**, share the room code.</Step>
  <Step title="Watch and talk">Friends paste the code into **Paste room ID**. Start the video call in the side panel.</Step>
</Steps>

<Install position="mid" />

**Pros:** your own account and settings, captions and quality as usual, free video call. **Cons:** desktop Chrome only.

## 2. Room sites: paste a link

Sites like Watch2Gether give you a room with a shared, synchronized player. You paste YouTube links into it and everyone watches there.

**Pros:** nothing to install, works in mobile browsers, great for queueing lots of short videos. **Cons:** you watch in their player rather than on YouTube with your own account.

## 3. Discord activities

If your group is already in a Discord voice channel, Discord's watch-together activity can play YouTube for everyone in the channel.

**Pros:** no new app for Discord groups. **Cons:** only works inside Discord.

## 4. Screen sharing

Share a YouTube tab on any call. Everyone else gets a compressed copy, so it's fine for one short clip and tiring for anything long. See [why](/blog/watch-party-vs-screen-sharing/).

## Watching with one person far away?

The same setup works for two: create a party, send the code, start the call. For planning around time zones, see the [long-distance movie date guide](/blog/long-distance-movie-date/).
