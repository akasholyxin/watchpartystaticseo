There's no single best watch party app. The right one depends on **how you want to talk**, **which devices** your group uses, and **where the video is**. This guide matches each situation to a tool, using facts from each product's own website (checked on the date above).

## Quick picks

| You need… | Best fit |
|---|---|
| Video calls for free, on desktop | Watch Party, Kosmi |
| Phones or tablets | Watch2Gether, Kosmi |
| Quick YouTube rooms | Watch2Gether |
| Safari or Edge | Teleparty |
| Discord groups | WatchParty.me (Discord bot) |
| Watching your own streaming accounts at full quality | Sync tools: Watch Party, Teleparty, Scener |

## First: two kinds of watch party tools

- **Sync tools** keep play, pause and seek in step while everyone plays the video on their own account. Full quality, no black screens, but everyone needs access to what you watch.
- **Shared-room tools** play one browser or player for the whole room. Easy to start and some work on phones, but you watch the room's stream, not your own.

## The apps

### Watch Party (Chrome extension)
Sync on any site with a standard video player, with **free group video and voice calls**, chat and reactions in a side panel. Public rooms in Explore, friends and messages. **Limits:** desktop Chrome only.

<Install position="mid" />

### Teleparty (browser extension)
The best-known Netflix-era extension. **Free:** sync and text chat on its free channels. **Premium:** voice and video chat, more channels, custom reactions. Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari.

### Watch2Gether (website)
Make a room, paste links, watch in a shared synchronized player. Best with YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion and SoundCloud. No account needed, works in mobile browsers, with chat and webcam.

### Kosmi (website and app)
Free rooms for watch parties and game nights, with voice, video and chat, integrations for services like Netflix and YouTube, plus shared browsing and screen sharing. Mobile app available.

### Hyperbeam (website and desktop app)
A shared "multiplayer" browser that runs on their servers, with chat and calls. Good for browsing sites together.

### WatchParty.me (website)
Rooms with a cloud browser, YouTube, screen sharing, file upload or a video URL, plus video chat and a Discord bot. No registration.

### Scener (website)
Describes itself as a free way to watch shows and movies from major streaming platforms socially, in sync.

## Built-in watch parties are disappearing

Streaming services have been removing their own watch party features: **Disney+ GroupWatch ended in 2023**, and **Prime Video's Watch Party** was removed later. If you relied on those, a separate tool is now the way to keep watching together.

## How to choose, in four questions

1. **Where will you watch?** Your own streaming accounts → a sync tool. YouTube links → a room player.
2. **Do you want to see each other?** Check that calls are on the free plan.
3. **What devices?** Desktop browser extensions don't run on phones or TVs.
4. **Test it with one friend** before the big night.
