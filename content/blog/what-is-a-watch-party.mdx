A **watch party** is a group of people watching the same thing at the same time and reacting together. It can be in person (fans watching a match at a bar) or online, where friends in different places watch the same video in sync and talk over chat or a video call.

## Online watch parties: how they work

1. **Everyone opens the same video** on their own device and account.
2. **A watch party tool keeps it in sync.** When the host plays, pauses or skips, everyone's video does the same.
3. **You react together** through chat, reactions, or a voice or video call.

Because everyone streams the video themselves, it plays at full quality for each person. That's the big difference from [screen sharing](/blog/watch-party-vs-screen-sharing/), where one person sends a compressed copy.

## "Watch party" on popular platforms

| Platform | Built-in watch party today? | How people watch together |
|---|---|---|
| Netflix | No | A browser extension that syncs everyone's own account |
| YouTube | No sync for regular videos | An extension, or a room site you paste links into |
| Discord | Watch-together activities in voice channels | Activities for YouTube; extensions for streaming services |
| Disney+ | GroupWatch ended in 2023 | A separate watch party tool |
| Prime Video | Its Watch Party feature was removed | A separate watch party tool |
| Twitch | Prime Video watch parties on Twitch ended in April 2024 | A separate watch party tool |

For a service not listed, check its own help pages for built-in features.
