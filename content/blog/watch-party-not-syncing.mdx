Most sync problems have the same handful of causes, whichever watch party tool you use. Run the 60-second triage first; if that doesn't do it, jump to your symptom.

## The 60-second triage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Everyone refreshes the video page">Fixes pages that loaded before the extension was ready, or after it updated.</Step>
  <Step title="Check it's the same video">Same title, same episode, same page. A trailer or the wrong season looks like a sync problem.</Step>
  <Step title="Check everyone can play it">Signed in, has the subscription, and the title is available in their country.</Step>
  <Step title="Leave and rejoin the room">Then press play again.</Step>
</Steps>

## One person keeps falling behind

- **Slow connection:** let the video buffer for a few seconds, or lower the quality in the streaming site's settings.
- **Too many tabs or apps:** close heavy tabs, especially other videos.
- **Ads:** if only that person gets an ad, they'll be behind until it ends.
- **VPN:** a VPN can change the region and the title, or slow everything down. Turn it off and refresh.

## The sound doesn't match the picture

This is almost always the player or the device, not the watch party:

- Pause and play again, or refresh
- **Bluetooth headphones** add delay; try wired ones or the laptop speakers
- Update your browser and close heavy tabs

## A friend can't join

- They need the **same watch party extension** installed in a supported desktop browser
- Have them paste the **full room code or link**
- They must be **signed in to the streaming service**, with access to the title
- Make sure they're on **desktop**: browser extensions don't run on phones or TVs

## The service's own watch party disappeared

Some services removed their built-in watch parties: **Disney+ GroupWatch ended in 2023** and **Prime Video's Watch Party** was removed later. If that's what stopped working, a separate watch party extension is the way to keep watching together.
