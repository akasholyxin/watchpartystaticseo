We make a different watch party extension, so this isn't official Teleparty support. These fixes come from how browser watch party extensions work in general and solve most problems. For account or billing issues, contact Teleparty directly.

Start with the symptom you're seeing.

## The Teleparty icon is grey, missing, or does nothing

1. **Open a video page first.** The extension only becomes active on a supported video page, not on the service's home screen.
2. **Refresh the page** after opening it. If the page loaded before the extension was ready, it won't connect.
3. **Pin the extension:** click the puzzle icon in the toolbar and pin it.
4. **Check it's enabled** in your browser's extensions page, and update the browser.
5. **Use a supported desktop browser.** Teleparty lists Chrome, Edge and Safari.

## Your friend can't join the party

- They need the **extension installed** in a supported desktop browser
- They must be **signed in to the same streaming service** with access to the title
- The title has to be **available in their country**
- Send a **fresh party link** and have them open it in the same browser where the extension is installed

## The video is out of sync

1. **Everyone refreshes** the video page and rejoins.
2. Check everyone is on **exactly the same episode**.
3. Let a slow connection **buffer for a few seconds**, or lower the video quality in the service's settings.
4. **Turn off other video or ad extensions** that change the player, then refresh.

The same checks fix most sync problems in any tool: see [watch party not syncing](/blog/watch-party-not-syncing/).

## It doesn't work on Prime Video, Hulu or Disney+

- Make sure **everyone can play the title** themselves, on their own account and in their region
- Open the **episode page itself**, then start the party
- Check the service is included in **the plan you're on**: Teleparty's free plan covers its free channels, and some services need Premium

## There's no video chat

On Teleparty, **voice and video chat are a Premium feature**. The free plan has sync and text chat.
