**The short version:** pick a time that works in both of your time zones, open the same movie on your own accounts in desktop Chrome, keep it in sync with a watch party extension, and start a video call so you can see each other react. When one of you pauses, it pauses for both.

## What you need

- **A computer each** with desktop Chrome
- **Access to the movie on each account** (both of you need the service, and the title has to be available in both countries)
- **A watch party extension with video calls.** {{brand}} is free and has the call built in, so you don't need a second app.
- **Headphones** so the film doesn't echo into the call

## Pick a time that works for both of you

The hardest part of a long-distance movie date is usually the clock, not the tech. Look for the overlap where it's evening for one of you and still a reasonable hour for the other.

| You're in | They're in | Try | Your time | Their time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New York | London | Weekday evening | 3:00 pm | 8:00 pm |
| London | Mumbai | Weekend | 3:30 pm | 8:00 pm |
| Mumbai | New York | Weekend morning (US) | 7:30 pm | 10:00 am |
| Los Angeles | Sydney | Your evening | 7:00 pm | 1:00 pm next day |
| Berlin | Toronto | Weekday | 9:00 pm | 3:00 pm |

Times shift with daylight saving, so double-check the week you plan. Save long films for the weekend and shorter ones for weeknights.

## A date plan: before, during, after

**Before (15 minutes):** dress up a little, make or order the same food, and send each other a photo of your setup.

**During:** keep the call small so the film stays the star, use chat or reactions for the funny bits, and pause for a snack break together.

**After (the best part):** leave the call running when the credits roll and talk about it. That's half the date.

## 12 long-distance movie date ideas

1. **Rewatch your first movie** together
2. **Swap picks:** you choose this week, they choose next week
3. **Same dinner, same movie:** cook the same recipe first
4. **Trailer roulette:** watch five trailers, pick one on the spot
5. **Start a series** you only watch together
6. **Decade night:** only movies from the year you met
7. **Each other's childhood favorites**
8. **A movie set in their city**
9. **Rate it out of 10** in chat at the end, the winner picks next time
10. **Themed snacks** that match the film
11. **Double feature** on a weekend
12. **Dress code night:** match the movie's vibe

## What about phones and TVs?

{{brand}} is a Chrome extension, so it works on computers only. If one of you only has a phone, either use an app built for mobile, or keep a video call open on your phones and count down to press play on the TV. Counting down works, but you'll drift out of sync every time someone pauses.
