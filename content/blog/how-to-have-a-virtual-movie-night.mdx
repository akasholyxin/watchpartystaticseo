A virtual movie night works when two things are handled: **the tech disappears** (everyone's video stays in sync without anyone counting down) and **it feels like an event** (a time, a theme, a reason to stay for the credits). This playbook covers both.

## The run-of-show

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| A few days before | Vote on the movie in the group chat, pick a theme, set the time |
| Day of | Send the room code and a reminder with the start time in everyone's time zone |
| 5 minutes before | Video call on, snacks out, quick catch-up |
| The movie | Host drives; chat for running commentary; pause for one snack break |
| After | Keep the call going: rate it out of 10, pick next week's host |

## Theme ideas

- **Director double feature:** two films, vote on the better one
- **Decade night:** only the 80s, 90s or 2000s
- **So bad it's good:** the lowest-rated movie you can find
- **Franchise in order:** one per week
- **Childhood favorites:** everyone nominates one, spin to choose
- **Around the world:** a film set in each person's city or country

## Snacks, when you're not in one room

- Agree on a **snack theme** that matches the film
- **Send someone a snack** by delivery as a surprise
- **Same recipe challenge:** everyone makes it before, compare on camera

## Games that don't ruin the movie

- **Prediction cards:** three predictions each before it starts
- **Cliché bingo:** call them out in chat, first full line wins
- **Rate it:** score out of 10 at the end; the highest scorer picks next week
- **Quote pause:** pause once and everyone guesses the next line

## Tips by group size

- **2 people:** keep the video call visible, chat less; it's a date, see [long-distance movie date](/blog/long-distance-movie-date/)
- **3–8 people:** video call on, one person keeps chat lively
- **10+ people:** chat for everyone, call for a smaller circle, and mute when not talking

## Etiquette that keeps it fun

Mute when you're not talking, use chat during dialogue, and pause for breaks instead of making people miss scenes. Rotate who hosts and who picks.
