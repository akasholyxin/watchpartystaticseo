**The best way, if you're on computers:** everyone opens the same title on their own Netflix account in desktop Chrome, and a watch party extension keeps playback in sync; one with a built-in video call lets you see each other too. If someone's on a phone or TV, there are workable options below, with their trade-offs.

## Which method should you use?

| Your situation | Best method | Stays in sync? |
|---|---|---|
| Everyone on a computer | Watch party extension | Yes |
| Your group talks on Discord | Discord voice + watch party extension | Yes |
| You're on FaceTime or a phone call | Call on your phones + extension on your laptops | Yes |
| Everyone on phones only | Count down on a call, or a mobile watch party app | Mostly not |
| Watching on TVs | Count down on a call | No |

## On a computer: use a watch party extension

  <Step title="Everyone installs an extension">{{brand}} is free for desktop Chrome and includes a video call.</Step>
  <Step title="Open the same title">Each person signs in to their own Netflix account.</Step>
  <Step title="Create a party">The host clicks the extension icon, presses **Create Watch Party** and shares the room code.</Step>

## With Discord: talk on Discord, sync with an extension

Screen sharing Netflix in Discord usually shows your friends a **black screen**, because Netflix protects its video ([here's why](/blog/watch-party-vs-screen-sharing/)). Keep Discord for voice and let an extension keep everyone's own Netflix in sync.

## On FaceTime or a phone call

Talk on your phones and watch on your laptops with an extension, so the movie stays in sync while you chat. Watching *and* calling on the same phone works for a short episode, but you'll be counting down "3, 2, 1, play" and drifting apart every time someone pauses.

## On phones only, or on TV

Browser extensions don't run on phones or TVs. Your options:

- **Count down together** on a call and press play at the same moment. Fine for one episode; expect to resync after every pause.
- **A mobile watch party app.** Some tools have phone apps; see [best watch party apps](/blog/best-watch-party-apps/).
