**The method that works for any group:** everyone opens the same movie on their own account in desktop Chrome, a watch party extension keeps it in sync, and you add a video call or chat so you can react together. What changes with group size is how you run the room.

## Step 1: Make sure everyone can play the movie

| Where the movie is | What each person needs |
|---|---|
| A subscription service (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Hulu…) | Their own account, and the title available in their country |
| A movie you rent or buy | Their own rental or purchase |
| A free, ad-supported service | Access in their country; ads may break sync briefly |

We don't link to unofficial movie sites. Stick to legal services: they're safer and the quality is better.

## Step 3: Run the room for your group size

**2 people:** keep the video call visible; it's closer to a date. See the [long-distance movie date guide](/blog/long-distance-movie-date/).

**3–8 people:** video call on for everyone, chat for running jokes. Agree that anyone can ask for a pause.

**10–20+ people:** chat for everyone, video call for a smaller group, and mute when you're not talking so the movie stays audible. One person hosts and controls playback.
